Figure 1. Smooth-field transfer accuracy on structured quadrilateral (left) and unstructured mixed (right) target meshes. The ordinate is the relative nodal density L₂ error; h is the count-based effective spacing. NN, BAR, GP, and CV denote nearest-neighbor Taylor interpolation, barycentric interpolation, Galerkin projection, and control-volume transfer, with numerical suffixes indicating nominal accuracy order. Gray lines are first-, second-, and third-order slope guides, not fitted curves.

Figure 2. First-step lift-coefficient difference ΔC_L after transfer to the target Euler airfoil mesh, relative to the continuation of the target-mesh reference. NN, BAR, GP, and CV denote nearest-neighbor Taylor interpolation, barycentric interpolation, Galerkin projection, and control-volume transfer; suffixes indicate nominal accuracy order. For each method, the same error field is imposed in both stored states or in one state alone. The opposite signs of the responses with only the older state perturbed and with both states perturbed are consistent with the BDF2 coefficients.
