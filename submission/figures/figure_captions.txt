Figure 1. Smooth-field transfer accuracy on structured quadrilateral (left) and unstructured mixed (right) target meshes. The ordinate is the relative nodal density L₂ error; h is the count-based effective spacing. NN, BAR, GP, and CV denote nearest-neighbor Taylor interpolation, barycentric interpolation, Galerkin projection, and control-volume transfer, with numerical suffixes indicating nominal accuracy order. Gray lines are first-, second-, and third-order slope guides, not fitted curves.

Figure 2. First-step lift-coefficient difference ΔC_L after transfer to the target Euler airfoil mesh, relative to its native continuation. NN, BAR, GP, and CV denote nearest-neighbor Taylor interpolation, barycentric interpolation, Galerkin projection, and control-volume transfer; suffixes indicate nominal accuracy order. Within each method, the same spatial discrepancy is assigned to both histories or to one history only. The opposite signs of the older-only and both-histories responses are consistent with the second-order backward differentiation weights.
